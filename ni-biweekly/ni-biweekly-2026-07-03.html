Keane M, Sajan S, Nockels K, Zochios V, Ahmed A, Yusuff H
Journal of cardiothoracic and vascular anesthesia · Jul 2026
This systematic review examined 14 studies reporting 31 machine learning models built to predict outcomes, like mortality and complications, in critically ill patients receiving ECMO. Random forest and gradient boosting were the most common models, and half showed good to excellent predictive performance, but nearly all had high risk of bias and few were externally validated. These findings suggest ML tools show promise for ECMO decision support, but methodological gaps limit their readiness for clinical use.

This tool could help nurses spot infection risk earlier, supporting proactive, data-driven monitoring in vulnerable patients.

The Impact of Electronic Health Records on Nurses and Nursing Care in Low- and Middle-Income Countries: A Scoping Review.

Osman W, Asah-Ofori S, Harris A, Yakong VN, Widger K, Chu CH
Nursing open · Jul 2026
This scoping review examined 41 studies (2015–2024) on how electronic health records affect nurses and nursing care in low- and middle-income countries. EHRs improved documentation and data access, but studies also reported data quality issues, workflow disruption, technostress, and depersonalized care, with uneven research output across regions. The authors call for nurse-led research, especially in Sub-Saharan Africa, to guide context-sensitive practice, education, and policy.
